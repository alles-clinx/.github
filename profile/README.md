# Alle's ClinX

**Official GitHub organization for Alle's ClinX — institutional hygiene systems for professional facilities in India.**

Alle's ClinX (also styled **Alle’s ClinX**) is a B2B institutional hygiene brand operated by **Champaran Innovatives Private Limited**. We combine professional cleaning chemistry with public safety documentation, operational guidance, procurement tools and AI-assisted product support for healthcare, education, hospitality and industrial facilities.

## What Alle's ClinX builds

### Professional hygiene chemistry
A focused range of institutional cleaning and hygiene chemicals for recurring professional use, supported by dilution guidance, Safety Data Sheets, operating procedures and product documentation.

### ClinXAi
[ClinXAi](https://allesclinx.com/clinxai/) is the digital intelligence layer around the Alle's ClinX product system, connecting domain-specific tools for procurement, chemistry and institutional operations.

### Nova
[Nova](https://allesclinx.com/about-nova/) is Alle's ClinX's proprietary AI assistant for product, safety-documentation and compliance questions grounded in the company's own documentation.

### Metricon
[Metricon](https://allesclinx.com/metricon/) is a public set of procurement, chemistry, dosage, cost and supplier-decision calculators.

### Alle's ClinX Plus
[Alle's ClinX Plus](https://allesclinx.com/plus/) connects recurring institutional supply with planning and facility tools, including the [Supply Planner](https://allesclinx.com/plus/supply-planner/), [Hygiene Passport](https://allesclinx.com/plus/hygiene-passport/) and [Facility Tools](https://allesclinx.com/plus/facility-tools/).

## Principles

- **Evidence before claims.** Technical information should be testable, traceable and clearly communicated.
- **Safety before convenience.** Compatibility, PPE, storage and product-use limits belong close to the chemistry.
- **Repeatable operations.** Good hygiene depends on clear procedures and consistent execution.
- **Useful documentation.** Public technical resources should help operators, procurement teams, auditors and safety professionals make better decisions.
- **Built for institutional use.** Products and tools are designed around professional facility workflows rather than consumer cleaning.
