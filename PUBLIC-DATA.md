# Public Data Policy

Alle's ClinX publishes selected technical and operational information openly to improve document discoverability, procurement clarity and machine readability.

## Intentionally public

The public repository may include:

- product names and SKUs;
- pack-size information;
- published pH ranges;
- shelf-life information;
- links to public SDS, TDS, SOP and usage guides;
- facility hygiene checklists, logs and templates;
- document indexes and revision metadata;
- public organization identifiers;
- machine-readable references to official Alle's ClinX resources.

The curated machine-readable dataset is available under [data/](./data/).

## Not intentionally public

The repository should not be used to publish:

- proprietary formulations or manufacturing recipes;
- non-public raw-material specifications;
- supplier pricing or confidential supplier relationships;
- customer records or usage data;
- confidential commercial terms;
- credentials, secrets or access tokens;
- private employee information;
- unpublished internal quality-control records.

## Source of truth

The official Alle's ClinX website and current controlled product documentation remain the authoritative sources for product and safety decisions.

Website: https://allesclinx.com/

Document Center: https://allesclinx.com/support/media-documents/

## Corrections

If public structured data differs from the current controlled document, the controlled document takes precedence. Report discrepancies to **care@allesclinx.com**.
