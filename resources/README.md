# Free Facility Hygiene Resources

Alle's ClinX public operational-resource library for facility, housekeeping, safety, training, audit and procurement teams.

The library contains **50 free operational resources** plus the master resource-library index PDF and CSV. Resource groups include:

1. Core cleaning operations
2. Chemical management & safety
3. Equipment & housekeeping control
4. Training & competency
5. Audit & continuous improvement
6. Consumption & procurement
7. Sector-specific resources

These resources are practical operating aids and should be adapted to the facility's own risk assessment, infection-prevention, food-safety, legal and management requirements where applicable.
