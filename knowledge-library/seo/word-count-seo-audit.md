## Word-count production rules

| Article type | Core usefulness | SEO expansion |
|---|---:|---:|
| Explainer | 900–1,400 | 700–1,100 |
| Comparison | 1,000–1,500 | 800–1,200 |
| Troubleshooting | 900–1,400 | 700–1,100 |
| Guide | 1,200–1,800 | 900–1,400 |
| Reference | 1,000–1,500 | 900–1,300 |

Use the lower end when the question is narrow and fully resolved. Use the upper end when the page genuinely needs examples, comparison tables, definitions, decision criteria, FAQs, or supporting context. Never pad an article to hit a number.

## Publish gate for every article

- One clear primary search intent.
- H1 and SEO title aligned, but not forced to be identical.
- Unique meta description.
- Search intent fully answered without filler.
- Descriptive H2/H3 structure.
- At least 2–4 useful contextual internal links where relevant.
- Category and subcategory breadcrumb links.
- Canonical URL matches registry.
- Existing Alle's ClinX page overlap checked.
- Claims supported by appropriate sources; product-specific operational guidance defers to controlled documentation where required.
- Final article passes duplicate-intent and keyword-stuffing review.
